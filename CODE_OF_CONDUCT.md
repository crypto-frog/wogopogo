# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, ability, or perspective.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate with patience, clarity, and professional respect
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or confidential information
- Make room for newcomers and explain project-specific context
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and operational risk

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and public interactions when someone is representing Wogopogo.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a private conduct report, use the repository's private vulnerability reporting form and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. Maintainers will review reports confidentially, respond proportionately, and may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, issue warnings, or restrict participation.

Maintainers must avoid conflicts of interest and protect the privacy and safety of everyone involved as far as reasonably possible.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and common open-source community standards.
