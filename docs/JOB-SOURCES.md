# Public job sources for Wogopogo

Use this discovery register with `WEEKLY-REFRESH.md`. These are starting points, not a feed
to copy wholesale and not a claim that every listed role is open. Verify the individual job,
local work location, application route, original date and deadline during each run.

| Publisher / channel | Starting point | Coverage and verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City of Kelowna | https://careeropportunities.kelowna.ca/postings/search | Municipal, engineering, administration and technology. Individual pages show posting numbers, dates and application links. |
| Okanagan College | https://jobs.lever.co/okanagan | Education, administration, facilities, retail and IT across several campuses. Read closing dates inside the description; an Apply button alone does not prove an unexpired deadline. |
| Interior Health | https://jobs.interiorhealth.ca/ | Clinical, support, trades and office roles. Filter to supported local communities; inspect each competition's closing date and employment type. |
| Big White Ski Resort | https://www.bigwhite.com/employment-centre/jobs-at-big-white | Seasonal tourism, hospitality, retail and facilities. Follow Current Job Postings to the employer's ATS; the recruitment calendar is not an individual vacancy. |
| YMCA of Southern Interior BC | https://www.ymcasibc.ca/Join/Our-Team/Careers | Childcare, aquatics, recreation and administration. Follow the employer-linked public opportunities portal and check the actual location. |
| BGC Okanagan | https://bgcokanagan.bamboohr.com/careers | Childcare, family services and recreation. Individual pages require JavaScript; verify through a rendered browser when available. Without readable evidence, record unreachable and report for follow-up. |
| Other local employers and regional boards | Search the employer's official website and public careers links | Rotate municipalities, schools, wineries, farms, nonprofits and private businesses across the valley. Do not treat mirrored copies as distinct opportunities. |
| Job Bank | https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/jobsearch/ | Supplement coverage after the independent-source search; retain precise original attribution and application links. |

Use stable keys such as `kelowna:ck54284p`, `lever:okanagan:<posting-uuid>` or
`interiorhealth:<competition-number>`. Record evidence privately, outside the repository.
Respect site access restrictions; do not bypass authentication or CAPTCHAs. If one publisher
is unavailable, search another and report the gap rather than silently reverting to one source.

The public board should credit the source, show when it was checked, link to the original,
and invite employers to post directly for free. Do not describe sourced listings as employer
submissions, imply partnerships, or reveal private origin-control fields.
